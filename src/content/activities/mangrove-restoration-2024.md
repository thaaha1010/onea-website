## About This Project

Mangroves are one of the Maldives' most vital ecosystems — they protect coastlines, support fish populations, and store significant amounts of carbon. In 2024, ONEA partnered with local communities in Addu Atoll for a major mangrove restoration effort.

## What We Achieved

- Planted over 500 mangrove saplings across 3 restoration sites
- Trained 25 local community members in mangrove monitoring techniques
- Documented baseline biodiversity data at each site
- Engaged 15 local school students in hands-on environmental education

## Why Mangroves Matter

Mangrove forests in the Maldives face threats from coastal development, pollution, and climate change. Each mangrove tree we plant helps stabilize shorelines, provides habitat for juvenile fish, and contributes to local carbon sequestration.

## Ongoing Monitoring

ONEA volunteers continue to monitor the planted saplings quarterly, with a survival rate tracking above 85% at the six-month mark.
