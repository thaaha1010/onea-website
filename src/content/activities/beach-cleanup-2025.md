## About This Activity

Our annual Hulhumale Beach Cleanup is one of ONEA's flagship events, bringing together volunteers from all walks of life to clean and protect our beloved coastlines.

## Highlights

- Collected over 250kg of plastic waste and debris
- More than 90 volunteers participated from across Malé and Hulhumale
- Environmental awareness sessions conducted for local school students
- Partnered with local diving clubs for underwater cleanup segments

## Impact

This year's cleanup made a real difference to the health of Hulhumale's beach ecosystem. All collected waste was properly sorted and recycled where possible, with data collected to inform future environmental policy recommendations.

Thank you to everyone who joined us and helped make this event a success!
