## About This Workshop

This intensive two-day workshop is designed to equip young Maldivians with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to become environmental leaders in their communities.

## What You Will Learn

- Environmental leadership and advocacy skills
- Climate change science and local impacts on the Maldives
- Community organizing and project management
- Public speaking and communication techniques
- Coral reef conservation and monitoring basics

## Who Should Attend

This workshop is open to all youth aged 16–30 who are passionate about the environment and want to make a difference in their communities. No prior experience required — just enthusiasm and commitment!

## Schedule

**Day 1** — Environmental Science & Leadership Foundations
**Day 2** — Community Action & Project Development
